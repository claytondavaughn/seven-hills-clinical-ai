# Product requirements
Nurse workflow: admission establishes demographics, diagnosis, comorbidities (or explicit none), baseline and benefit history. Carry historical findings with dates, never assume current status. Quick confirmations unchanged/changed/not assessed/unable with reason; no preselected unchanged. Routine visits should not require recert-level evidence. Consolidate essential questions; max three optional nonblocking recommendations. Preserve stability, improvement, contradictions; never invent decline or certify eligibility. Cohesive narrative without form labels, separate interventions and warnings. Diagnosis-specific prompts for FAST, NYHA when applicable, pulmonary, cancer, malnutrition (dated weight/MAC/BMI/intake/function/wasting), and custom diagnoses; deduplicate overlapping prompts. Prototype fields are not universal Medicare mandates. Verify authoritative LCD and regulatory sources before asserting requirements.
Future workflows: voice dictation/transcription; IDG compares prior IDG, recert compares prior benefit period; CTI evidence summary supports physician independently composing certification narrative; separate NP F2F workspace with benefit history and timing; admin census, visit gaps, decline trajectories, eligibility/ADR risk, recert/F2F deadlines, SIA/HVLDL prospective and retrospective metrics. No automatic discharge, unnecessary visits or unvalidated mortality prediction. Clinical review required. Versioned clinical knowledge library. No real patient data until security, clinical validation and applicable BAAs are complete.
